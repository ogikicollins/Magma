## Two ICPs — Different Sequences Required

**ICP A — Rails Staff Augmentation:**
- Target: CTOs / VPs Engineering at Series A-C SaaS companies (20-200 employees)
- Signal triggers: new CTO hire (first 90 days), engineering team job posts, Series A/B funding close
- Message angle: "Your engineering team has a gap. MagmaLabs fills it with senior Rails engineers in 2 weeks."
- Channel: LinkedIn + email

**ICP B — AI Services (Code Cleanup / Prototype Studio):**
- Target: Founders and CTOs who have posted publicly about AI integration challenges
- Signal triggers: LinkedIn post mentioning "AI," "LLM," "integrate AI into our stack," Rails + AI job posts
- Message angle: "You're trying to bolt AI onto a Rails codebase. MagmaLabs has done this 12 times in the last 6 months."
- Channel: LinkedIn DM + email

**Key SELLL build for MagmaLabs:**
- 4 sequences total (2 per ICP)
- LinkedIn signal monitoring: daily alerts for trigger events in both ICPs
- Inbound conversion layer on magmalabs.io
- Cold email + LinkedIn outreach (no cold calling — dev agency ICP responds to written channels)
